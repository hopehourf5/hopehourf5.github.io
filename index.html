Who we are

Born out of love and compassion for humanity.

Hope Hour Foundation was inspired by the everyday struggles people face and the difference a small act of kindness can make. Our vision is to spread love, offer encouragement to the broken-hearted, give hope to the living, and become a shoulder humanity can lean on.

We believe compassion should move beyond words. By building awareness, encouraging practical care, and bringing people together, we hope to help create communities where no one is treated as invisible.

Hope Hour Foundation is currently building awareness and developing its initiatives. Please contact us to discuss collaboration or support. Do not interpret this website as a claim of formal registration or tax-exempt status.

Meet the Founder

Joel Agada

Founder, Hope Hour Foundation


My name is Joel Agada, a trained Tourism Management Technology professional from Isa Mustapha Agwai I Polytechnic, Lafia, Nasarawa State, Nigeria. I am the visionary Founder of Hope Hour Foundation, an initiative born out of a deep compassion for humanity and a profound sense of divine purpose discovered during my academic journey.


My passion for humanitarian service is rooted in the belief that every human life deserves dignity, love, compassion, and hope, regardless of background or circumstance. I believe that beyond our daily struggles, there is always an opportunity to make a difference in someone's life through kindness, understanding, and practical support.


Hope Hour Foundation is committed to promoting education, supporting healthcare needs, providing food assistance to vulnerable individuals, and advancing other humanitarian initiatives that improve lives and strengthen communities.


My vision is to help build a world where no one feels forgotten, where the vulnerable are treated with dignity, and where compassion inspires meaningful action. Although the foundation is currently in its early development and awareness-building stage, I remain committed to growing this vision through partnerships, community engagement, volunteers, and the support of people who share the same passion for humanity.


Guided by faith in God and the principles of love, service, and compassion, I believe that even the smallest act of kindness can restore hope and create a lasting impact.


My guiding principle is simple: Humanity comes first.


Together, we can extend hope, touch lives, and build a more compassionate world—one person, one community, and one act of kindness at a time.
